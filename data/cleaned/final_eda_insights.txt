1. Dataset contains 19,685 records covering 55 crops, 30 states and 6 seasons from 1997 to 2020.
2. Coconut  recorded the highest average yield among crops (8,652.00).
3. Coconut  recorded the highest total production (308,751,970,278.00).
4. Goa recorded the highest average yield among states (354.78).

6. Whole Year  recorded the highest average yield among seasons (413.00).
7. Whole Year  recorded the highest total production among seasons (314,452,748,974.00).
8. Production showed the strongest positive correlation with Yield (0.5708).
9. IQR-based outlier analysis identified extreme observations in several agricultural variables. These observations were retained because extreme values can represent genuine differences in crop area, production, rainfall or input usage.
